Diamond price analysis by cut, colour, and clarity

Key Findings

Carat weight and price have a strong positive correlation (0.922).

Premium diamonds have the highest average price among cut categories.

J-colour diamonds have the highest average price among colour categories.

SI2 diamonds have the highest average price among clarity categories.

These findings describe patterns in this dataset and do not establish that any single characteristic causes a higher price.
